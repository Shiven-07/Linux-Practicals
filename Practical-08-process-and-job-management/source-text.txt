PRACTICAL 9 
Process Management 
Aim 
To monitor, analyse and control Linux processes. 
 
View processes 
ps 
ps aux 
ps -ef 
 
Find a process 
ps aux | grep ssh 
 
Process tree 
pstree 
If unavailable: 
ps --forest 
 
top 
top 
Press: 
q 
to exit. 
 
 
 
Identify resource-intensive processes 
ps -eo pid,user,%cpu,%mem,comm --sort=-%cpu | head 
Memory: 
ps -eo pid,user,%cpu,%mem,comm --sort=-%mem | head 
 
Kill Process

Find PID: 
pgrep sleep 
Terminate: 
kill PID 
If necessary: 
kill -9 PID 
Use kill -9 only when normal termination does not work.
